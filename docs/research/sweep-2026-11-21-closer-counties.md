# Closer-counties sweep: Ogemaw, Gladwin, Arenac, Clare (2026-11-21)

Why: the earlier sweep's best leads (Frederic, Lake Township) sit well past the 2.5-hour preference. These four counties are the closest part of the research area. Every finding below comes from an ordinance or official page read on 2026-11-21; quotes and section numbers are in `data/sources.json`. Contacts went to the private outreach file only.

Drive times are OSRM free-flow times to the township's interior point. They run about 15–25% long, so 145–160 routed minutes is roughly 2 to 2¼ hours in practice.

## Restrictive or unclear

- **Whitney (Arenac):** tents and RVs may not be used for dwelling purposes in residential districts, except during construction or as guest quarters at an existing house. Privies prohibited.
- **Edwards (Ogemaw):** no accessory building until a main building exists. Camping not addressed (archive copy, amendments to 2009).
- **Gladwin county-zoned (10 townships):** an owner may use an RV as a dwelling up to six months a year only if hooked to an approved well and septic. Short-term tent camping is not addressed. Rated `unverified` pending a county answer.
- **Tobacco (Gladwin):** an RV occupied 3+ months counts as a permanent dwelling; nothing on tents.
- **Au Gres (Arenac):** camping rules only for Lakeshore Residential (tents 7 days a year); the posted file is labelled a 2024 draft.
- **Lincoln (Arenac):** 2001 ordinance does not address camping.

## Across all four counties

- **Yurts** are not addressed anywhere.
- **Tents** are the recurring gap again: most RV sections name only motor homes and trailers.
- **Outhouses** are banned by Ogemaw County zoning, Billings (residential), Hayes and Whitney.
- **Health department:** Ogemaw is District Health Department #2; Gladwin, Arenac and Clare are Central Michigan District Health Department. Its sanitary code was not read (site gave no readable text).
- Archive copies (energyzoning.org) were used for Edwards, West Branch Township, Billings, Clayton and Turner because the township sites had no readable copy. Each record carries a conflict note asking to confirm the ordinance is current.
