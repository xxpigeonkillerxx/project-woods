# Missaukee County sweep (2026-11-20)

All 15 civil townships: Aetna, Bloomfield, Butterfield, Caldwell, Clam Union, Enterprise, Forest, Holland, Lake, Norwich, Pioneer, Reeder, Richland, Riverside, West Branch. The cities of Lake City and McBain are zoned and are not in the atlas.

Result: every township is now `researched`, `unzoned_reported`, camping outlook still `unverified`. No posted ordinance says an owner may or may not camp on their own vacant land. **Unzoned does not mean camping is allowed.** The next step is a short written question to each clerk, plus one county-level question to District Health Department #10.

Public file: no contact details here. Contacts live in the private project folder (`outreach/contacts.csv`).

## County-level findings (apply to every township)

| Topic | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Zoning | "All local municipalities in Missaukee County administer their own planning and zoning regulations. However, only the City of Lake City and the City of McBain have zoning regulations in place." | 2021 Missaukee County Guide to Permitting & Zoning, p. 4 |
| Zoning (conflict) | The county's ordinance page still lists Ord. 68-Z-1 and 1974–75 amendments to the **Missaukee County Interim Zoning Ordinance** (adopted Aug 20, 1973), with no repeal listed. 75-Z-3 (Oct 16, 1975) rezoned land in Aetna, Bloomfield, Butterfield, Caldwell, Clam Union, Lake, Richland and (West) Norwich. Whether any of it is still in force is unknown. Flagged as a `conflict_note` on every record. | county ordinances page; 75-Z-2, 75-Z-3 |
| Interim zoning text on RVs | 75-Z-2 Sec. 3.1.02B.2 (R-1-B): "Recreational Vehicle or mobile homes during the course of construction of a dwelling on a lot, subject to the provisions of section 12.14F." No tent or camping provision. | 75-Z-2 |
| Mobile homes | Ord. 93-2 (Oct 12, 1993): mobile homes used as residences or rentals must meet HUD standards and pass county inspection. Does not mention RVs, travel trailers, tents or camping. | 93-2 |
| Building code | Ord. 98-1: the County Building Official enforces the State Construction Code; the County "assumes responsibility for the administration and enforcement of said Act throughout its corporate limits." | 98-1 |
| Sanitation | DHD#10 Sanitary Code (rev. Jan 25, 2019) covers Missaukee (Sec. 1.03). Sec. 2.06: a "dwelling" includes "any house, building, structure, tent, water craft, shelter, trailer or vehicle" used permanently or transiently. Sec. 4.11: habitable buildings need approved water and sewage. Sec. 5.02(19): privies must be watertight vault type, serve one dwelling, sit 100 ft from other buildings and 10 ft from lot lines; Sec. 5.02(10) requires a permit for any sewage disposal system. No camping-specific rule found. Chapters 5 (end) through 10 not yet read verbatim. | DHD#10 code |
| Public land | Master plan: "A large portion of northern Missaukee County is stateowned land. Approximately fifty (50%) percent of some townships are state owned property." | 2014 master plan |

## Questions for the county (not a township record)

- Missaukee County Planning: is the 1973 Interim Zoning Ordinance (and its 1975 amendments) still in force anywhere, or was it superseded when the county stopped zoning?
- District Health Department #10: for owner tent or camper use on a vacant parcel with no dwelling, is a portable toilet or a permitted vault privy acceptable, and does Sec. 2.06's "tent ... used transiently" trigger the water and sewage requirements?
